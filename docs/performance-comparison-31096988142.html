TestMu Standard

Conclusion

  • BrowserStack had the best pass rate: 11/15.
  • TestMu HyperExecute passed 10/15.
  • TestMu Standard passed 9/15.
  • HyperExecute was significantly faster than Standard for both suites.
  • Both TestMu modes continue to show issues in Fresh SRP, Perps, and seedless flows.
  • Predict Available Balance, Predict Deposit, and most basic startup flows passed across all providers.

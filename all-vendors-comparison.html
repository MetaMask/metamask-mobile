  • Both lanes 16/23 passed after Appium settings alignment (waitForIdleTimeout: 0, snapshotMaxDepth: 62, etc.).

Quick conclusions

  • HyperExecute still leads on pass count in the prior baselines (18/23).
  • Sauce Private & Cloud are now tied at 16/23 and much closer to TestMu on Fresh SRP.
  • Private remains the stabler Sauce lane for launch/warm and Import SRP; Cloud is competitive on Fresh SRP / Google / Perps add funds.
  • Tests without metrics should not be read as app slowness — they indicate flow/session/instrumentation failure.

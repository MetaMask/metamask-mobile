6. Conclusions

  • BrowserStack leads same-run reliability (20/23) and is the only lane measuring Perps open/close + Predict Deposit in 32736700431.
  • TestMu HyperExecute remains strongest among prior baselines (18/23) and still leads Warm → Login / Asset View historically, but Fresh SRP stays a QG fail.
  • Sauce Private (17/23) edges Cloud (16/23). Both clear Fresh SRP; Private is the stabler Sauce lane (Cloud adds Seedless Google + Cold → Login fails).
  • On the 11-scenario shared timed-pass set, Sauce Private has the best mean (3.61s), then Cloud (3.89s), BS (4.08s), HE (4.26s), Standard (4.43s).
  • For fair vendor comparison: prefer same-run data (BS + Sauce from 32736700431) and ignore Money Home / Rewards until they stop returning no metrics everywhere.
